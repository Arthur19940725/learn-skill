## Integrated 90-Minute Session

```markdown
# Observable learning target

## 5 minutes: SQ3R survey and questions
- Write six questions covering the research question, method, evidence, result, limitations, and implications.

## 25 minutes: Focus block 1
- Read or study to answer the questions.
- Capture answers and evidence in the Cornell main-notes column.

## 5 minutes: Break

## 25 minutes: Focus block 2
- Solve, apply, or build something.
- Add evidence and uncertainties to the notes.

## 5 minutes: Break

## 15 minutes: Feynman teach-back
- Close all sources.
- Explain the idea and identify gaps.

## 10 minutes: Review setup
- Put the six SQ3R questions and any weak-point prompts in the Cornell cue column.
- Update chunk status.
- Schedule the next retrieval.
```
